02 / THE APPROACH

Good on the surface.
Solid underneath.

Design and development belong in the same conversation. The goal is a website people can understand, use, and remember.

See the project-based CV
01

Give the page a purpose.

Start with the audience, the offer, and the one action that matters. Agree the scope before building.

02

Make the details earn their place.

Clear type, deliberate color, and interactions that work with a mouse, a keyboard, and a thumb.

03

Test it. Then hand it over.

Check the real flows and screen sizes. Deliver the source with practical setup notes. Development uses AI assistance with browser checks.
